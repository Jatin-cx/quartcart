Shipping & Returns

Standard delivery timetables, courier tracking, and our 30-day return guarantee.

Shipping & Delivery

We fulfill orders Monday through Friday from our regional fulfillment centers. Standard shipping is complimentary on all orders exceeding ₹999 within India.

Delivery Estimates

  • Standard Domestic Delivery: 3 to 5 business days.
  • Express Priority Courier: 1 to 2 business days.
  • International Standard: 6 to 12 business days (subject to customs clearance).

30-Day Return Guarantee

If an item does not meet your expectations, you may return it within 30 days of confirmed delivery for a refund to your original payment method.

Return Conditions

  • Items must be unused, unwashed, and in original packaging with all included documentation.
  • Include the original order confirmation or packing slip inside the return parcel.
  • Refunds are processed within 3 to 5 business days following inspection at our fulfillment facility.

Initiate a Return

To initiate a return, contact our support team at support@quickcart.com or phone +91 98765 43210 with your order reference.
