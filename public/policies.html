30-Day Concierge Returns

Every object in Quartcart is guaranteed for material integrity. If an acquired item does not meet your personal standard of excellence, we provide a 30-day return window from the moment of signature delivery.

Eligibility Criteria

  • All pieces must remain unworn, unwashed, and in pristine original presentation.
  • Original serialized product packaging, warranty certificates, and protective foils intact.
  • Accompanied by the original digital Quartcart order receipt.

Return Logistics

Contact our team at concierge@quartcart.com to receive a prepaid priority courier dispatch label.

Privacy Protocol

Your identity and telemetry are held under stringent zero-knowledge principles. We do not sell, rent, or trade client dossiers under any circumstances.

Data Safeguards

  • Card data processed strictly via Stripe Level 1 PCI-DSS tokenization.
  • Passcodes salted with bcrypt (cost factor 12).
  • JSON Web Tokens expire automatically after 7 calendar days.

Terms of Acquisition

Due to limited drop allocations and artisan production pipelines, orders are fulfilled strictly on a first-confirmed basis. All prices are listed in USD and inclusive of standard import duties.
