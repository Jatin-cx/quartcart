Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 10, 2026. Last Updated: October 10, 2026.

1. Introduction

Quartcart ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website, create an account, make a purchase, or interact with our services.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information that identifies or reasonably relates to you, including:

  • Account Information: Name, email address, and encrypted credentials when registering an account.
  • Order and Delivery Data: Shipping address, recipient name, phone number, and order history.
  • Payment Information: All payments are processed directly by Stripe. Quartcart does not collect, store, or have access to your full credit card number or card security codes. Payment processing is governed by Stripe's Privacy Policy.
  • Technical and Device Data: IP address, browser type, operating system, and standard server log entries collected automatically for platform security and debugging.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your personal data strictly for legitimate operational purposes:

  • To process orders, verify transactions, and arrange shipping and courier delivery.
  • To authenticate customer accounts and maintain platform security.
  • To provide order tracking updates, customer support, and system notifications.
  • To comply with statutory tax, accounting, and legal requirements.

4. Data Sharing and Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. We share necessary data only with trusted infrastructure providers bound by strict confidentiality and data protection obligations:

  • Payment Gateways: Stripe, Inc., for PCI-DSS compliant payment processing and fraud prevention.
  • Database and Hosting Infrastructure: Cloud hosting facilities for database storage and secure transmission.
  • Courier Partners: Shipping carriers solely for delivering packages to your designated address.

5. Cookies and Local Storage

We use essential local storage items (such as your shopping cart state and session authentication token) required for basic storefront functionality. We do not use third-party behavioral advertising tracking cookies.

6. Data Security and Retention

We employ modern industry security protocols, including HTTPS/TLS 1.3 encryption across all network transfers, cryptographic password hashing (bcrypt), and role-based access limits. Personal data is retained only for the duration necessary to satisfy customer order fulfillment, accounting records, or legal compliance.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction (such as under the GDPR or CCPA), you may have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data held by Quartcart. To submit a data request, contact us through our administrative channels.

8. Contact Information

If you have questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact our support team at:
